How this score works

The score starts at 50. It adds up to 20 points for kept promises and up to 30 for resolved reflections. It subtracts up to 30 for unresolved high-impact reflections and 20 for currently broken promises.

Each adjustment is proportional to its share of the selected reflections or promises. Empty groups contribute zero. This is a personal reflection aid. Try recording a lesson or completing one practical promise.
